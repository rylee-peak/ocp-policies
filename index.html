Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2025

1. Introduction

The Office of Commissioner Rylee Peak ("the Office") respects your right to privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you provide to us through our digital platforms, in correspondence, or during public engagement.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical address when you sign up for newsletters, submit feedback, or contact our office for constituent services.
  • Automatically Collected Information: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and website browsing patterns collected via standard analytics tools to improve our digital services.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for official government purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and providing constituent services.
  • Distributing official updates, newsletters, and public safety announcements.
  • Analyzing web traffic to improve accessibility and user experience.

4. Public Records Notice

Please be aware that as a government entity, much of the correspondence sent to the Office of Commissioner Rylee Peak—including emails and submitted forms—may be considered a public record under state and federal transparency laws. Such records may be subject to disclosure upon request by third parties, unless exempted by law.

5. Security

We implement robust, industry-standard security measures designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Policy

Last Updated: March 15, 2026

1. Core Commitment

Commissioner Rylee Peak and the entirety of the Office believe that our district's diversity is its greatest strength. We are firmly committed to fostering an inclusive environment that respects, values, and celebrates the unique backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives of all residents and employees.

2. Equity in Public Service

We are dedicated to ensuring equitable access to government resources, services, and opportunities. This means actively identifying and dismantling systemic barriers that have historically marginalized specific communities based on race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, disability, religion, or age.

3. Inclusive Workplace

As an employer, the Office of Commissioner Rylee Peak enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy for discrimination and harassment. We actively prioritize diverse hiring practices, equitable pay, and continuous cultural competency training for all staff members.

4. Community Engagement

True representation requires listening to all voices. We commit to actively seeking input from underrepresented communities when formulating public policy, planning district projects, and allocating public funds. Translation services and accessible venues are standard requirements for all public Office events.

Accessibility Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2025

1. ADA Compliance Commitment

The Office of Commissioner Rylee Peak is committed to ensuring that our facilities, programs, and digital platforms are accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities, in compliance with Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

2. Digital Accessibility

We are continuously working to make our website conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA standards. This includes ensuring compatibility with screen readers, providing alt-text for images, maintaining high color contrast, and enabling keyboard navigation.

3. Requesting Accommodations

If you require an accommodation to participate in a public meeting, access a document, or utilize our services, please contact our ADA Coordinator at least 72 hours prior to the event or requested service date.

  • Email: accessibility@ryleepeakoffice.gov
  • Phone: (555) 019-8273
  • TTY/TDD: 711 (Relay Service)

4. Feedback and Grievances

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on our website or within our offices, please report it immediately so we can address the issue. Formal grievances regarding ADA compliance can be filed directly through our office portal.

Ethics & Conduct Policy

Last Updated: February 1, 2026

1. Standard of Integrity

Public service is a public trust. Commissioner Rylee Peak and all staff members are held to the highest standards of ethical conduct, transparency, and accountability. We pledge to make decisions based solely on the public interest.

2. Conflicts of Interest

No employee or official within the Office may use their position for private gain. All staff must disclose any potential financial or personal conflicts of interest regarding pending legislation, contracts, or district projects. Affected individuals must recuse themselves from the decision-making process.

3. Gift Ban

To prevent undue influence, the Office enforces a strict zero-dollar gift ban. Staff and the Commissioner are prohibited from accepting gifts, meals, event tickets, or favors from lobbyists, contractors, or any entities seeking business with the government.

4. Anti-Retaliation (Whistleblower Protection)

The Office encourages the reporting of waste, fraud, and abuse. We strictly prohibit any form of retaliation against employees or members of the public who report ethical violations in good faith.

Public Records Requests

Last Updated: August 10, 2025

1. Commitment to Transparency

The Office of Commissioner Rylee Peak believes that an informed public is essential to a functioning democracy. We comply fully with all state and federal Freedom of Information Acts (FOIA) and Public Records laws.

2. How to Submit a Request

Public records requests must be submitted to the official Records Custodian. To expedite your request, please be as specific as possible regarding the dates, subjects, and types of documents you are seeking.

Requests can be submitted via:

  • Online Portal: Available at records.ryleepeakoffice.gov
  • Email: records@ryleepeakoffice.gov
  • Mail: Records Custodian, Office of Commissioner Rylee Peak, 100 Civic Center Drive, Suite 400.

3. Response Times and Fees

We will acknowledge receipt of your request within 5 business days. While we strive to provide records electronically at no cost, voluminous requests that require significant staff time or physical printing may incur statutory fees. You will be provided with a cost estimate before any charges are incurred.

4. Exemptions

Certain records may be redacted or exempt from disclosure under the law to protect individual privacy, active investigations, or sensitive security information. Any redactions will be accompanied by a legal justification.
